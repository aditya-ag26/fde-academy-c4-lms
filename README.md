## 📥 Hand in your work

<table>
<tr>
<td width="60%" valign="top">

### → [Discussions **▸ Assignments**](../../discussions)

**One thread per submission.** Open a new discussion, pick the
**Assignment submission** form, and put the activity id in the title:

```
[A01] Keyword baseline
```

The form asks for your **approach before your result** — on purpose. An approach
written afterwards is a reconstruction of one.

The bot replies within a minute to confirm it registered. That reply is your receipt.

**Revising after feedback?** Reply in the *same* thread with stage `Revision`. Never
open a second one — your review history lives in the first.

</td>

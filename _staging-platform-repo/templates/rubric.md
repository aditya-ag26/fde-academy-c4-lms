## For reviewers

<!-- Under peer review this is read by students. Tell them how to give feedback:
     what to look at first, what to leave alone, and how to say something is
     wrong without discouraging the person who wrote it. -->

- Read the **approach** before the code. Review the thinking first.
- Name one thing that works before naming what does not. It is not politeness —
  it tells the author which parts to keep.
- Be specific: "the retry loop has no cap" is actionable, "error handling could
  be better" is not.
- You are not marking. You are telling them what you saw.

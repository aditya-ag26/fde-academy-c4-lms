# 📊 How to give Feedback

**For:** pace · difficulty · content · sessions · assignments
**Not for:** ❓ doubts → Q&A · 🛠️ problems → Help Desk

- 🎯 One topic per post
- 🔧 Suggest a fix, not just a complaint
- 👍 Agree with a post? Upvote it, don't repeat it
- 👀 Public and under your name. Stay respectful
